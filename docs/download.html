All releases

What's new

1.8

Home-screen widgets

  • 繼續收聽 — the playing episode with back-15s / play-pause / next buttons; larger sizes also list what plays next.
  • 最近播放 — recently played shows; one tap resumes the whole show where you stopped.
  • 我的節目 — a cover wall of favorited programs and subscribed podcasts; pick and order which shows appear.
  • 學習紀錄 — minutes listened today, learning streak, and this week's total.

Resume everywhere

  • Every episode remembers its playback position and picks up where you stopped; finishing an episode clears it so a re-listen starts fresh.
  • A new recently-played list — separate from favorites — tracks the shows you're working through.

1.7

App icon

  • New "Tune In" icon — white headphones with a smile on the orange gradient, with themed and legacy variants.

Programs & podcasts

  • Language filter chips fold into a single row, and the selected filter persists across launches.
  • The New Podcast dialog links to searching the show on Apple Podcasts.

1.6

Playback & player

  • Audio no longer stalls when the screen turns off, and tapping the playback notification opens the app.
  • Streamed audio is cached with a 2 GB cap (least-recently-used eviction).
  • Player startup is more robust, and several player-sheet performance fixes.

AI jobs & sync

  • 重試 restarts a failed transcript or handout job; duplicate transcription requests share one job.
  • Translations keep interior blank lines so bilingual rows stay aligned.
  • Drive sync no longer cancels in-flight work and paginates large libraries completely.

Stability

  • Crash fixes, atomic record writes, orderly PDF/handout viewer teardown.
