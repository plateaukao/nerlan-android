Learn a language by listening — and actually studying.

NerLan streams the language courses of Taiwan's National Education Radio (國立教育廣播電臺) and your own podcasts, then turns every episode into study material: an AI transcript synced to the audio, a review handout, translations, and shadowing practice.

What it does

Radio courses & podcasts

The full NER catalog of language courses — Japanese, Korean, English, and more — plus any show you add from Apple Podcasts or an RSS feed.

Transcripts that follow along

One tap transcribes an episode into clean sentences. The line being spoken stays highlighted and centred while you listen.

AI study handouts

A review sheet per episode: summary, grammar points, example sentences, and a vocabulary table with pronunciation.

Translation, line by line

Translate the transcript into your language and read it beside the original — or on its own.

Shadowing practice

Loop a sentence a set number of times, record your own reading, and play it back to compare.

Offline, synced, on your home screen

Download episodes with their PDF handouts, auto-cache what you stream, sync study material through Google Drive, and keep widgets for daily practice.

A look around

NerLan is open source. Course content is provided by 國立教育廣播電臺 (National Education Radio); podcasts come from their own feeds.
